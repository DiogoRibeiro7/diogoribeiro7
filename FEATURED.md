# Featured Projects

Twelve repositories for reviewers who want evidence quickly: a mixed cross-section of production-style systems, statistical software, empirical studies and decision models. It is not a ranking. Maturity labels are descriptive: a production-style system shows engineering design, not live deployment; a package or CI is not proof of statistical correctness; an empirical association is not a causal effect.

For problem-to-outcome narratives see [Case Studies](CASE_STUDIES.md).

## Production and systems

| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **[feedback-intelligence-agent](https://github.com/DiogoRibeiro7/feedback-intelligence-agent)** | production-style system | RAG service with retrieval evaluation, guarded generation, FastAPI serving, observability and CI. |
