## Industry Outcomes

In industrial manufacturing I built demand forecasts for 60 products and 80 international customers, inventory and logistics analytics (moving reporting from spreadsheets to SQL), and stochastic heat-transfer models for cork disinfection.

Reported results: 12% better forecast accuracy, 40% fewer inventory days, about €500K less inventory value, 25% less obsolete inventory, 18% lower logistics costs, 30% lower analytics processing time, 80% lower reporting costs. These are historical industry figures; the commercial data and baselines are not published here and do not describe the public repositories.

## How I Work

- **Question first.** Define the estimand, constraints and cost of a wrong decision before choosing an algorithm.
- **Earn complexity.** Strong baselines; validation that respects time, groups and censoring.
- **Show uncertainty and failure.** Calibration, drift, leakage and abstention, not only aggregate scores.
- **Stay inspectable.** Tests, provenance and frozen configurations.
