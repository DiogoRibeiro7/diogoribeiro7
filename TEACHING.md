# Teaching

I teach mathematics and data subjects at the **Faculty of Media Arts and Design, Technical University of Porto (FMAD–UTP)**. Course material is kept as versioned, testable, reusable repositories (exercises, notebooks, tests, reference implementations) rather than files that disappear after a semester.

## Courses and material

| Course / activity | Focus | Material |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Mathematics I** | Logic, sets, differential and integral calculus, from symbolic work to computational intuition. | [calculus-with-python](https://github.com/DiogoRibeiro7/calculus-with-python) · [esmad_public](https://github.com/DiogoRibeiro7/esmad_public) |

## Academic presentations

**[academic-presentations](https://github.com/DiogoRibeiro7/academic-presentations)** is a source-first LaTeX/Beamer library with **20 active compiled course decks plus standalone tracks**, built on a shared FMAD–UTP theme and validated in CI. Topics: statistical learning, optimisation, deep and reinforcement learning, MCMC and Bayesian ML, causal inference and A/B testing, forecasting, explainable AI, AI agents, streaming pipelines, software testing. Many modules include code, exercises, assessment material and reproducible builds. [Slide previews](https://diogoribeiro7.github.io/academic-presentations/)

## Approach

- Start from the mathematical or statistical object; tools follow definitions and assumptions.
- Pair theory with code, numerical examples or counterexamples.
- Assess reasoning, evidence and interpretation, not only the final number.
- Automate structural feedback; keep conceptual assessment human.
- Teach uncertainty and failure, not only the happy path.

## Seminars, mentoring and supervision

Sessions connect a mathematical or statistical idea to an operational workflow: modelling and experimentation, causal inference, forecasting and anomaly detection, Bayesian methods, MLOps, graph analytics, sensor systems, scientific ML, LLM/RAG evaluation.
